02 — WHO WE ARE

Electronic Safety & Security Solutions Design Around Your Environment

Established in 2017 as a subsidiary of RIANA GROUP, Unified Secure Systems (USS) is a leading provider of electronic safety and security solutions, with a notable presence in East Africa. As an industry pioneer, USS has garnered a reputation for excellence in designing, implementing, and maintaining innovative security systems tailored to the unique needs of businesses and organizations.

Our commitment to innovation and client satisfaction drives our continuous evolution in meeting the demands of the market. Leveraging our expertise and strategic partnerships, we offer end-to-end security solutions that mitigate risks and ensure regulatory compliance in the Kenyan and broader African market.
